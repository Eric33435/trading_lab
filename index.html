How I Trade

Trading Strategy

Order flow scalping on US index futures. I read the live auction using Bookmap and Footprint charts to detect when large passive participants are absorbing aggressive flow — then trade the reaction.

Core Philosophy

Use Order Flow to find high win-rate entries
Fast in, fast out — no overnight holds
Execute with discipline, never with emotion

Entry Conditions — All 3 Must Align

1

News Bias

▲Bullish news → look for long setups
▼Bearish news → look for short setups
◆Neutral → both directions valid
2

CVD Divergence

↓Price above trendline + CVD flattening/falling → short
↑Price below trendline + CVD recovering → long
★Stronger divergence = higher win rate
3

Absorption Confirmation

●Large green volume = buy absorption → long
●Large purple volume = sell absorption → short
★Larger volume = more reliable signal

Bonus Conditions (optional, but better)

Option Chain Max Pain support/resistance
Call Wall / Put Wall key levels
Round numbers (7,400 / 7,450 / 7,500)

Seller Absorption at Support

Bookmap Advanced

Reversal Confirmed — Buyers In Control

CVD flips to +242 as sell aggression dries up. The footprint shows ask-side imbalances stacking at the low — buyers are hitting the offer aggressively, confirming the reversal is underway.

Absorption Intermediate

Buy Absorption Reversal — DAX

Large bid volume stacks at the bottom rows of the footprint while price holds the low. Red delta confirms sell pressure, yet the level refuses to break — classic buy absorption before a sharp bounce.

Absorption Advanced

Delta Divergence Before Reversal — NQ

Price prints a lower low but delta diverges — footprint reveals ask imbalances stacking at the low. Sellers are failing to push through; passive absorption morphs into a short-squeeze setup.

Delta Intermediate

High-Volume Rejection Candle — S&P 500

A spike candle with massive volume at the wick — the footprint reveals exhaustion selling. Delta turns green after the spike, marking the reversal entry zone with near-perfect precision.

Delta Intermediate

Bearish Delta Divergence — S&P 500

Price grinds higher inside a rising wedge while the delta indicator trends lower — buyers are quietly weakening. This hidden divergence precedes the sharp breakdown visible on the right of the chart.

Imbalance Beginner

Bullish Delta Recovery — S&P 500

After a sharp sell-off, delta flips positive while price is still at the low — aggressive buyers step in before price reacts. Waiting for this delta confirmation is the entry trigger I use before going long.

Delta Advanced

False Breakout Identification — DAX

High volume breakout but delta doesn't confirm — buyers aren't actually stepping in aggressively. This divergence between price and delta is the key tell for identifying a false breakout before the reversal.
